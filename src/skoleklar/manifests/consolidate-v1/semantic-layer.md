Consolidation operates after source derivation. Input items are individually
evidence-backed; this layer decides identity between those items, not whether their
source statements are true.

## Resolutions

| Value | Meaning |
| --- | --- |
| `duplicate` | Two or more items restate the same semantic fact without adding material detail. |
| `complementary` | Items describe the same fact and contribute compatible details. |
| `revision` | A newer item explicitly replaces or updates an older representation. |
| `conflict` | Items appear to describe the same fact but disagree on date, scope, obligation, or another material field. |

Related does not mean identical. A school event, registration deadline, payment,
thing to bring, and transport instruction remain separate items even when they
refer to the same occasion. Likewise, two actions by the same child stay separate
when each can be completed independently.

The canonical item is the phone-sized representation of one consolidated fact.
Its semantic fields retain the meanings defined by the `school-items-v1` derivation
taxonomy. `reason` explains why the identities match; it is audit information and
not delivered to the family. Review is required for conflicts and any uncertain
identity or canonical field.
