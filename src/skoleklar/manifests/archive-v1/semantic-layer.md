Archival removes an item from the active working set without deleting its derivation,
sources, workflow history, or audit record. It is reversible. This review is only for
items that the deterministic policy cannot safely close.

`archive` means the item is no longer actionable or relevant after `as_of`.
`retain` means the item may still matter or its status cannot be established safely.
Past dates are evidence, but parent actions can remain outstanding after a deadline;
retain those unless the text clearly binds the action to a completed occasion.
